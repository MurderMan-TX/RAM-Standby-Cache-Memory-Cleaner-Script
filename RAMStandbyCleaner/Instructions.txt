Go to powershell as admin and do this:

cd C:\Users\YOURUSERNAMEHERE\Downloads\

powershell -NoExit -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "RAMStandbyCleanerV3.ps1" -Once

powershell -NoExit -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "RAMStandbyCleanerV3.ps1" -IntervalSeconds 300


Creating a shortcut on your Task bar:
Right-click RAMStandbyCleanerV3.ps1 → New → Shortcut

Then Right click on the shortcut and go to Properties.

Update this as the Target path:
powershell.exe -NoExit -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Path\To\RAMStandbyCleanerV3.ps1" -IntervalSeconds 300

Example:
powershell.exe -NoExit -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File "C:\Users\YOURUSERNAME\Downloads\RAMStandbyCleaner\RAMStandbyCleanerV3.ps1" -IntervalSeconds 300

Replace C:\Path\To\ with the actual location of the script then click OK to save and apply the changes to the shortcut.

Right Click > Pin to Task Bar
